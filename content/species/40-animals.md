# UK animals: hazards, food (where legal) and signs. Most UK wildlife is protected by law.

=== adder
name: Adder
latin: Vipera berus
group: animal
kind: reptile
status: venomous, protected
season: Active Mar–Oct; basks on sunny mornings in spring
habitat: Heathland, moorland, rough grassland, woodland edges, sand dunes, bracken slopes, across mainland Britain (not Ireland)
summary: The UK's only venomous snake: grey-cream or brown with a bold dark zig-zag along the back. Bites are rare and rarely fatal, but always need hospital.
lookalikes: grass-snake, slow-worm
tags: snake, viper, venom, bite, zigzag
wiki: Vipera berus
---
!danger If bitten
Call **999** (or 112). Keep the person still and calm, with the bitten limb as still as possible. Remove rings, watches and tight clothing near the bite before swelling starts. **Do not** cut, suck, apply ice or use a tourniquet. See [[a:adder-bite]].
!end

## Identification
- Thick-bodied, usually 50–65 cm.
- A **dark zig-zag stripe** down the back. Males are grey-cream with a black zig-zag; females brown with a dark brown zig-zag. Some are all black.
- A V- or X-shaped mark on the head; **red eye with a vertical slit pupil**.

## Behaviour
- Shy. It bites only if trodden on or handled. Watch where you put your hands and feet on sunny banks, and wear boots.
- It is illegal to kill or injure an adder.

=== grass-snake
name: Grass snake
latin: Natrix helvetica
group: animal
kind: reptile
status: protected, info
season: Active Mar–Oct
habitat: Near ponds, rivers, ditches, wet meadows and gardens in England and Wales
summary: Harmless olive-green snake with a yellow and black collar behind the head. Swims well; plays dead or releases a foul smell if handled.
lookalikes: adder
tags: snake, harmless, collar, swimming
wiki: Natrix helvetica
---
## Identification
- Long (up to 1.5 m), slender; **olive-green to grey** with dark bars along the sides.
- **Yellow/cream and black collar** behind the head; round pupils.
- No zig-zag stripe.

## Behaviour
- Non-venomous. If threatened it hisses, strikes with its mouth closed, releases a foul-smelling fluid, or plays dead. Leave it alone; it is protected.

=== slow-worm

kind: reptile
status: protected, info

habitat: Grassland, heaths, gardens, woodland edges, under logs and stones

- 30–45 cm, **smooth, glossy**, golden-brown, bronze or grey; females have a dark stripe down the back and dark sides.

=== tick
name: Sheep tick
latin: Ixodes ricinus
aka: deer tick, castor bean tick
group: animal
kind: arachnid
status: caution
season: All year, peak Mar–Oct
habitat: Long grass, bracken, heather and woodland, especially where there are deer or sheep; also urban parks
summary: Tiny blood-sucking arachnid that waits on vegetation and can transmit Lyme disease. Remove it promptly and correctly.
lookalikes: 
tags: lyme disease, borrelia, bite, deer, bracken
wiki: Ixodes ricinus
---
## Identification
- Nymphs are the size of a poppy seed; adults like a sesame seed. Engorged females swell to the size of a pea and turn grey.
- Eight legs (adults and nymphs). They are found attached in warm skin folds: behind the knees, groin, armpits, waistband, hairline and behind the ears.

## What to do
- Remove it as soon as possible with a tick tool or fine-tipped tweezers: grip close to the skin and pull steadily upwards, without twisting hard or squeezing the body. See [[a:tick-removal-lyme]].
- Watch the site for a spreading red rash ("bullseye") over the following 3–30 days, or flu-like illness.

=== common-wasp
name: Common wasp
latin: Vespula vulgaris
group: animal
kind: insect
status: caution
season: Apr–Oct (worst Aug–Sep)
habitat: Everywhere; nests in the ground, banks, hollow trees, lofts
summary: Black-and-yellow social wasp that can sting repeatedly. Ground nests disturbed while gathering firewood or sitting down cause multiple stings.
lookalikes: hornet
tags: sting, anaphylaxis, nest, insect
wiki: Vespula vulgaris
---
## Identification
- 12–17 mm, bright yellow and black bands, narrow "wasp waist", few hairs.
- A stream of wasps going in and out of a hole in a bank or the ground is a nest: move away.

## What to do
- Move away calmly from a nest. One sting: wash, cold compress, painkillers. Signs of anaphylaxis (swelling of the face, lips or throat, wheeze, faintness) mean **999**: see [[a:stings-anaphylaxis]].

=== hornet
name: European hornet
latin: Vespa crabro
group: animal
kind: insect
status: caution
season: Apr–Nov
habitat: Woodland, parkland, hollow trees, mainly in southern England
summary: Large brown-and-yellow wasp (up to 3.5 cm). Less aggressive than common wasps away from the nest, but its sting is more painful.
lookalikes: common-wasp
tags: sting, wasp, insect
wiki: European hornet
---
## Identification
- Much bigger than a common wasp; **chestnut-brown and dull yellow**, with a reddish-brown head and thorax.
- Flies at dusk and is attracted to lights.

## What to do
- Treat stings as for wasps; watch for anaphylaxis. Report the black-legged, darker Asian hornet (an invasive species) when you can.

=== highland-midge
name: Highland midge
latin: Culicoides impunctatus
group: animal
kind: insect
status: caution
season: May–Sep, worst Jun–Aug at dawn and dusk
habitat: Damp, still, shady upland areas, especially in western Scotland
summary: Tiny biting fly that swarms in still, damp, dull weather. Not dangerous, but miserable; pick breezy, open camps.
lookalikes: 
tags: midges, bites, scotland, insect
wiki: Culicoides impunctatus
---
## Identification
- 1–3 mm grey flies in dense clouds that bite exposed skin.

## What to do
- Midges can't fly in winds above about 7 mph (11 km/h) or in bright sun: camp on breezy, open ground away from still water and bracken. Cover up; use a head net; smoke from a fire deters them.

=== red-deer
name: Red deer
latin: Cervus elaphus
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: caution, info
season: All year; the rut Sep–Nov
habitat: Scottish Highlands and islands, Exmoor, the Lake District, the New Forest, Thetford, and parts of East Anglia and the Peak District
summary: The UK's largest land mammal. Stags are dangerous during the autumn rut. Venison may only be taken by authorised stalkers.
lookalikes: roe-deer
tags: deer, venison, stag, rut, tracks
wiki: Red deer
---
## Identification
- Large (stags up to 1.3 m at the shoulder), red-brown in summer, greyer in winter, with a buff rump patch and short tail.
- Stags have large branching antlers (Aug–Apr) and **roar** in the rut.
- Tracks: two pointed "slots" up to 9 cm long.

## Safety and law
- Keep well away from stags in the rut, and from hinds with calves (May–Jul). Calves found lying alone are not abandoned: leave them.
- Deer can only be shot by authorised people with suitable firearms and permission, in the open seasons. Poaching is a serious crime. Deer carry ticks.

=== wild-boar
name: Wild boar
latin: Sus scrofa
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: caution
season: All year; piglets Feb–Jun
habitat: Forest of Dean (largest population); small populations in southern England and Scotland
summary: Bristly, dark wild pig that is shy but dangerous if surprised, cornered, or with piglets, or if a dog approaches.
lookalikes: 
tags: pig, boar, rooting, tracks
wiki: Wild boar
---
## Identification
- Large, dark grey-brown, bristly pig with a long snout; piglets are striped like humbugs.
- Signs: large patches of ploughed-up ground (rooting), wallows, and rubbing trees caked with mud.

## Safety
- Keep dogs on a lead. If you meet boar, especially sows with piglets, back away slowly and give them a way out. Never feed them.

=== badger
name: Badger
latin: Meles meles
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: protected, info
season: All year (nocturnal)
habitat: Woodland edges, hedgerows and farmland across the UK
summary: Black-and-white striped face, grey body, nocturnal. Heavily protected; its setts and paths are good signs of the land.
lookalikes: 
tags: sett, tracks, nocturnal, protected
wiki: European badger
---
## Identification
- Grey, stocky, low-slung, with a white face crossed by **two black stripes**.
- Setts: large D-shaped holes with big spoil heaps of soil and old bedding. Latrines: shallow dug pits with droppings.
- Tracks: broad pad with **five toes in a line** and long claw marks.

## Law
- The Protection of Badgers Act 1992 makes it illegal to kill, injure or take a badger, or to interfere with a sett.

=== red-fox
name: Red fox
latin: Vulpes vulpes
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: info
season: All year
habitat: Everywhere, from mountains to cities
summary: Rusty-red dog-like animal with a white-tipped bushy tail. Raids food stores; its tracks run in a neat straight line.
lookalikes: 
tags: fox, tracks, scavenger, food store
wiki: Red fox
---
## Identification
- Orange-red coat, white chest, black "socks" and ears, long bushy tail with a white tip.
- Tracks: neat oval prints with four toes and claws, in a straight line. Strong musky scent; screaming calls in winter.

## Notes
- Hang food bags and rubbish out of reach. Foxes can carry mange and tapeworms: don't handle them, and wash your hands after handling droppings.

=== rabbit
name: European rabbit
latin: Oryctolagus cuniculus
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: edible, caution
season: All year
habitat: Grassland, field edges, dunes, heaths and woodland edges across the UK
summary: Common grazing mammal living in warrens. Edible, but taking it is governed by strict law, and it is too lean to live on.
lookalikes: brown-hare
tags: rabbit, game, warren, snares, tracks, meat
wiki: European rabbit
---
## Identification
- Grey-brown, 40 cm, with long ears (without black tips) and a white tail flashed when running.
- Signs: burrow entrances, small round droppings in piles (latrines), runs through grass and nibbled turf.

## Law
- You may only take rabbits with the **landowner's or occupier's permission**, using legal methods. Snares are banned in Wales and Scotland and tightly controlled elsewhere; self-locking snares are illegal everywhere. See [[a:trapping-law]].

## Food notes
- Cook thoroughly (diseases include tularaemia, rarely, and parasites). Wear gloves to paunch. Rabbit is extremely lean: living on it alone causes "rabbit starvation" (protein poisoning). Eat it with fat and carbohydrate.
- Don't eat animals that look sick or have swollen eyes (myxomatosis).

=== brown-hare

habitat: Open farmland and grassland in lowland Britain

- Bigger and leaner than a rabbit, tawny, with **black-tipped ears** and amber eyes.

=== signal-crayfish
name: Signal crayfish
latin: Pacifastacus leniusculus
group: animal
kind: crustacean
status: edible, invasive
season: All year, most active in summer
habitat: Rivers, streams, canals, lakes and gravel pits across England, Wales and parts of Scotland
summary: Large invasive American crayfish, with a white-turquoise patch at the claw hinge and red claw undersides. Edible, but trapping requires authorisation.
lookalikes: white-clawed-crayfish
tags: crayfish, invasive, shellfish, trapping
wiki: Signal crayfish
---
## Identification
- Up to 16 cm or more; brown to blue-brown, with **smooth, large claws, red underneath**, and a **white or turquoise patch at the claw joint** (the "signal").

## Law
- England: trapping requires written permission from the Environment Agency **and** the landowner, using approved traps. It is an offence to keep, move or release live non-native crayfish. Rules are similar in Wales and Scotland.

## Food
- Boil thoroughly. Clean equipment and boots afterwards so you don't spread **crayfish plague**, which kills native crayfish.

=== weever-fish
name: Lesser weever
latin: Echiichthys vipera
group: animal
kind: fish
status: venomous
season: Mostly Jun–Sep
habitat: Buried in sand in shallow water on sandy beaches, especially in southern and western Britain at low tide
summary: Small sandy fish that buries itself in shallow water, with venomous spines on its back. Treading on one is agony: treat with hot water.
lookalikes: 
tags: sting, beach, venom, hot water
wiki: Echiichthys vipera
---
## What to do
- Immerse the foot in water **as hot as the person can tolerate** (test it with your own hand: hot but not scalding) for at least 30 minutes, until the pain eases. The venom is broken down by heat.
- Remove any visible spines with tweezers. Painkillers. Get medical help if pain persists, or there is swelling, signs of infection, or a severe reaction.
- Prevention: wear shoes or shuffle your feet in shallow water.

=== lions-mane-jellyfish
name: Lion's mane jellyfish
latin: Cyanea capillata
group: animal
kind: jellyfish
status: venomous
season: Jun–Sep
habitat: Coastal waters, especially northern and western Britain
summary: Large red-brown jellyfish with masses of long trailing tentacles. Painful stings, even from dead ones on the beach.
lookalikes: 
tags: jellyfish, sting, sea, beach
wiki: Lion's mane jellyfish
---
## What to do
- Get out of the water. Rinse the area with **seawater** (not fresh water).
- Remove any tentacles with tweezers or the edge of a bank card.
- Soak in, or apply, very warm water for at least 30 minutes, then take painkillers.
- Don't use vinegar or urine, and don't rub the area. Call **999** for difficulty breathing, chest pain, or a large area stung.

=== mussel
name: Common mussel
latin: Mytilus edulis
group: animal
kind: shellfish
status: edible, caution
season: Traditionally Sep–Apr (cooler months)
habitat: Rocky shores, piers and estuaries, attached in beds between the tides
summary: Blue-black shellfish in beds on rocks and piers. Edible, but shellfish poisoning and sewage pollution are real risks.
lookalikes: 
tags: shellfish, seashore, coast, food
wiki: Blue mussel
---
!warn Shellfish can poison
Mussels filter seawater, so they concentrate sewage bacteria, viruses and algal toxins (paralytic shellfish poisoning). These toxins are **not destroyed by cooking**. Only gather from clean open coasts, away from outfalls, harbours and estuaries, and not after heavy rain or during algal blooms.
!end

## Identification
- Elongated blue-black shell up to 10 cm, attached by fibrous "beard" threads.

## Food
- Gather live mussels that are tightly closed or close when tapped. Discard any that are cracked or stay open.
- Purge them in clean seawater, then boil or steam until they open, and discard any that **stay shut**.

=== cattle
name: Cattle
latin: Bos taurus
group: animal
kind: mammal
status: caution
season: All year; most danger when cows have young calves (spring)
habitat: Farmland, moorland, commons and footpaths
summary: Not wildlife, but cows with calves, and bulls, injure and kill walkers in the UK every year, especially people with dogs.
lookalikes: 
tags: cows, walking, footpath, dog, hazard
wiki: Cattle
---
## Safety
- Don't walk between cows and their calves. Move calmly and quietly around the edge of the herd.
- If cattle charge or surround you while you have a dog, **let the dog off the lead**: it can outrun them, and you are safer without it.
- Find another way round if you feel threatened, and don't run unless you have to.
